Muslim 360 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29th October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application (“Muslim 360”).

1. Information We Collect

We collect limited information, to provide accurate prayer timings, to maintain your account and saved settings, and to improve app stability.

a. Account Information

  • If you create an account, we store the details used to register and identify it — such as your email address or phone number, and your display name or profile photo if you provide one. The exact details depend on the sign-in method you choose.
  • We store the app preferences and content you save to your account, such as your selected city, prayer time calculation method, notification and adhan preferences, bookmarks, and reminders, so that they are available to you across sessions and devices.
  • You may delete your account and its data at any time. See our Account & Data Deletion guide.

b. Location Information

  • The App may access your device’s location (GPS) to calculate accurate prayer times based on your geographic area.
  • Location access is optional.
  • If you do not allow location access, you can manually search and select your city.
  • We do not store or retain precise GPS coordinates.

c. City Information

  • When you allow location access or manually select a city, the city name may be used for prayer time calculation.
  • City name data may be logged anonymously for analytics purposes to understand usage patterns.

d. App Usage & Diagnostics

We use Firebase and Mixpanel services to collect:

  • Anonymous app usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Anonymous product events (for example, video playback milestones) through Mixpanel.

This data helps us improve app reliability and performance.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information only to:

  • Calculate accurate prayer timings
  • Create and maintain your account, and sign you in
  • Save your preferences and content, and keep them available across your sessions and devices
  • Respond to your support requests and to account deletion requests
  • Understand general app usage trends
  • Identify and fix app crashes or technical issues

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We use Google Firebase and Mixpanel as our service providers, and data is processed on our behalf in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication and cloud database services store your account details, preferences, and saved content so the App can sign you in and restore your data.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process usage and diagnostic data solely for app analytics and stability.
  • Mixpanel processes anonymous product events on our behalf; no advertising identifiers are sent.

We may also disclose information where we are required to do so by law, or where it is necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, or a security incident.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Account information and the preferences and content you save are stored on secure Google Firebase infrastructure.
  • We collect only the personal information needed to operate your account and the App, and we do not store precise GPS coordinates.
  • All data transmitted is encrypted in transit using industry-standard security measures, and data at rest is encrypted by our infrastructure provider.
  • Access to account data is restricted to those who need it to operate and support the App.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account information and saved content for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and the associated data as described in our deletion guide.

  • Deletion requests are acknowledged within 7 days, completed within 30 days of verification, and purged from encrypted backups within 90 days.
  • A limited amount of data may be retained afterwards where the law requires it — for example, transaction and invoice records where a purchase was made. The full list and its retention periods are set out in the deletion guide.
  • Aggregated and anonymised statistics that cannot be linked back to you may be retained indefinitely.

To request deletion, see our Account & Data Deletion guide or email sahil.createx@gmail.com.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • You may deny location permission and manually select your city.
  • You may disable analytics collection through your device settings where supported.
  • You may review and update your profile details and preferences from within the App.
  • You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you by emailing us.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. See our Account & Data Deletion guide for what gets deleted, how long it takes, and how to request it.
  • You may request deletion of specific data without deleting your account.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

9. Contact Us
