FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Muslim 360 free?

Yes — prayer times, the Quran, and masjid discovery are free to use.

Does it work outside India?

Prayer times and the Quran work anywhere. Masjid discovery and announcements depend on coverage in your area.

How do I follow my masjid?

Search for your masjid in the app or open a share link from your masjid, then tap “Follow”.

How do masjid committees post announcements?

Committee members register in the app and, once approved, can post announcements and updates.
